**If something fails live:** the app works without Gemini (template text; Ask shows passages without an answer). Screenshots and the PDF brief are in `docs/screenshots/`.

**Likely questions:**
- *Why not a transformer?* We tested sentence embeddings; they tied (−0.01, CI −0.07 to +0.06). TF-IDF wins on explainability at equal accuracy.
- *Is the specificity score fair across companies?* It's rule-based and conservative: in two 30-passage spot-checks 48 of 60 tiers matched (done by the AI assistant, not a person), with misses in both directions. Each evidence passage shows which signals fired. Brand mentions count as "named", which favours Dabur's brand-by-brand MD&A. We say so in the model card.
- *Does the LLM make anything up?* It only rewrites computed figures and cites passages. Numbers are checked, and failing sections fall back to templates.
