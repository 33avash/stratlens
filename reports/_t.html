Theme shares measure disclosure emphasis: how much of each report's strategy narrative covers a theme. They say nothing about spending, execution or results, and are not a ranking. Passages come only from the Chairman/CEO letter and MD&A. A TF-IDF + logistic regression classifier assigned each passage a theme; shares are theme passages divided by all strategic (non-Other) passages. Year-over-year shifts count only when their 95% bootstrap interval excludes zero. Specificity counts concrete signals (figures, dates, named initiatives, commitments).
